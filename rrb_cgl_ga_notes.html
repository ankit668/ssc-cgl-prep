Historical EraCore Points & Associations
Indus Valley Sites• Lothal (Gujarat): Ancient tidal dockyard.
• Kalibangan (Rajasthan): Ploughed agricultural fields & fire altars.
• Mohenjo-daro: The Great Bath & Bronze Dancing Girl.
Buddhist Councils• 1st: Rajgriha (Ajatashatru, Mahakassapa).

• Lord Ripon: Known as the "Father of Local Self-Government" in India; repealed the Vernacular Press Act (1882).
Freedom Milestones• 1905: Swadeshi Movement against Bengal Partition.
• 1916: Lucknow Pact (INC & Muslim League joint session).
• 1920: Non-Cooperation Movement launched.
• 1930: Dandi Salt March (12 March – 6 April) launching Civil Disobedience.
• 1942: Quit India Movement ("Do or Die" call by Gandhi at Gowalia Tank, Bombay).
🌍 Indian & Physical Geography (202 PYQs Summary)
Physical, Rivers, Minerals
Geographical FeatureExact Exam Data
River Origins & Tributaries• Ganga: Originates as Bhagirathi from Gangotri glacier (Gaumukh); joins Alaknanda at Devprayag to form the Ganga.
• Indus Left-bank Tributaries: Jhelum, Chenab, Ravi, Beas, Satluj.
• Godavari (Dakshin Ganga): Originates at Trimbakeshwar, Nashik (Maharashtra); longest river in Peninsular India.
Strategic Mountain Passes• Zoji La: Connects Srinagar to Leh.
• Shipki La: Connects Himachal Pradesh to Tibet (Satluj river enters India through it).
• Nathu La: Connects Sikkim to Tibet.
• Palghat Gap: Major mountain pass in Western Ghats connecting Kerala (Palakkad) to Tamil Nadu (Coimbatore).
Mineral Belts• Coal: Jharia and Bokaro (Jharkhand) contain India's richest prime coking coal reserves.
• Iron Ore: Kudremukh & Bellary (Karnataka), Mayurbhanj (Odisha), Bailadila (Chhattisgarh).
• Mica: Koderma (Jharkhand) — historically known as the Mica Capital of the World.
Soils of India• Black Soil (Regur): Formed by weathering of basaltic lava; best suited for cotton cultivation; self-ploughing capacity.
• Alluvial Soil: Covers ~40% of India's land; richest in potash and lime, deficient in nitrogen and phosphorus.
📈 Economics & Financial Systems (149 PYQs Summary)
Macro, Planning, Banking
Concept / InstitutionExam Definitions & Facts
Five-Year Plans• 1st FYP (1951–56): Based on Harrod-Domar Model (agriculture focus).
• 2nd FYP (1956–61): Based on PC Mahalanobis Model (heavy industrialization focus).
• Plan Holiday (1966–69): Three annual plans due to Indo-Pak war and severe droughts.
Monetary Policy Tools• Repo Rate: Rate at which RBI lends short-term funds to commercial banks against government securities.
• Cash Reserve Ratio (CRR): Percentage of Net Demand and Time Liabilities (NDTL) that banks must keep in cash with RBI.
• Statutory Liquidity Ratio (SLR): Percentage of NDTL that banks must maintain in liquid assets (cash, gold, approved securities).
National Income Aggregates• GDP: Total market value of all final goods and services produced within domestic territory in a year.
• National Income technically refers to: Net National Product at Factor Cost (NNP at FC).
Inflation Indices• CPI (Consumer Price Index): Measures retail price change; base year 2012; published by NSO; anchor for RBI monetary policy.
• WPI (Wholesale Price Index): Measures wholesale price change; base year 2011-12; excludes services; published by Office of Economic Adviser.
