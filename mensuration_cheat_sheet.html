🔴 Advanced SSC Tier-2 Master Formulas

1. Prism & Pyramid (Generic)

If a question gives you a weird base (like a Hexagonal Pyramid or Triangular Prism): * Prism Volume: $\text{Base Area} \times \text{Height}$ * Prism LSA: $\text{Base Perimeter} \times \text{Height}$ * Prism TSA: $\text{LSA} + 2 \times (\text{Base Area})$ * Pyramid Volume: $\frac{1}{3} \times \text{Base Area} \times \text{Height}$ * Pyramid LSA: $\frac{1}{2} \times \text{Base Perimeter} \times \text{Slant Height}$ * Pyramid TSA: $\text{LSA} + \text{Base Area}$

2. General Triangle Rules

  • Inradius ($r$): $r = \frac{\Delta}{s}$ (Where $\Delta$ is Area, $s$ is semi-perimeter)
  • Circumradius ($R$): $R = \frac{abc}{4\Delta}$ (Where $a,b,c$ are sides)
  • Sine Rule: $\frac{a}{\sin A} = \frac{b}{\sin B} = \frac{c}{\sin C} = 2R$
  • Cosine Rule: $\cos A = \frac{b^2 + c^2 - a^2}{2bc}$

3. Number of Tiles / Revolutions

  • Number of Tiles required: $\frac{\text{Total Floor Area}}{\text{Area of One Tile}}$
  • Number of Revolutions of a Wheel: $\frac{\text{Total Distance}}{\text{Circumference} (2\pi r)}$

4. Polygon Formulas (n = number of sides)

  • Sum of Interior Angles: $(n - 2) \times 180^\circ$
  • Each Interior Angle (Regular): $\frac{(n - 2) \times 180^\circ}{n}$
  • Sum of Exterior Angles: $360^\circ$ (Always)
  • Each Exterior Angle (Regular): $\frac{360^\circ}{n}$
  • Number of Diagonals: $\frac{n(n - 3)}{2}$

❓ Top 5 Recent PYQs - Mensuration

[SSC CGL Tier-2 2023] The radius of a solid metallic sphere is 12 cm. It is melted and recast into small solid cones of radius 3 cm and height 4 cm. Find the number of cones formed. * Solution: Volume of Sphere = $n imes$ Volume of Cone. $ rac{4}{3}\pi(12)^3 = n imes rac{1}{3}\pi(3)^2(4)$ $4 imes 1728 = n imes 9 imes 4 \implies n = 1728 \div 9 = $ 192.

[SSC CHSL 2022] A wheel has a radius of 14 cm. How many revolutions will it make to travel 88 meters? (Take $\pi = 22/7$) * Solution: Circumference = $2 imes rac{22}{7} imes 14 = 88$ cm. Total Distance = $88$ m = $8800$ cm. Revolutions = $ rac{8800}{88} =$ 100.

[SSC CGL 2022] The sides of a triangle are 5 cm, 12 cm, and 13 cm. What is the inradius of this triangle? * Solution: Since $5^2 + 12^2 = 13^2$, it is a Right Triangle. Inradius $r = rac{b + h - d}{2} = rac{5 + 12 - 13}{2} = rac{4}{2} =$ 2 cm.

[IBPS PO 2023] The diagonals of a rhombus are 16 cm and 12 cm. Find its perimeter. * Solution: Side $a = rac{1}{2}\sqrt{d_1^2 + d_2^2} = rac{1}{2}\sqrt{16^2 + 12^2} = rac{1}{2}\sqrt{256 + 144} = rac{1}{2}\sqrt{400} = 10$ cm. Perimeter = $4a = 4 imes 10 =$ 40 cm.

[SSC CGL Tier-2 2021] Find the volume of the frustum of a cone whose height is 14 cm, and the radii of its upper and lower circular ends are 5 cm and 2 cm. * Solution: Volume = $ rac{1}{3}\pi h(R^2 + r^2 + Rr) = rac{1}{3} imes rac{22}{7} imes 14(5^2 + 2^2 + 5 imes2) = rac{44}{3}(25 + 4 + 10) = rac{44}{3}(39) = 44 imes 13 =$ 572 cm³.
