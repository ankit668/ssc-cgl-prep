📐 SSC CGL Trigonometry Cheat Sheet

1. Basic Ratios & Relationships

For a right-angled triangle with angle \( \theta \):

  • \( \sin \theta = \frac{Perpendicular}{Hypotenuse} \)
  • \( \cos \theta = \frac{Base}{Hypotenuse} \)
  • \( \tan \theta = \frac{Perpendicular}{Base} \)
  • \( \csc \theta = \frac{1}{\sin \theta} \)
  • \( \sec \theta = \frac{1}{\cos \theta} \)
  • \( \cot \theta = \frac{1}{\tan \theta} \)

3. Fundamental Identities

  • \( \sin^2 \theta + \cos^2 \theta = 1 \)
  • \( 1 + \tan^2 \theta = \sec^2 \theta \)   \( \Rightarrow \sec^2 \theta - \tan^2 \theta = 1 \)
  • \( 1 + \cot^2 \theta = \csc^2 \theta \)   \( \Rightarrow \csc^2 \theta - \cot^2 \theta = 1 \)

4. Advanced SSC Shortcuts (Highly Tested)

If \( A + B = 90^\circ \) (Complementary Angles):

  • \( \sin A = \cos B \)   and   \( \sec A = \csc B \)
  • \( \tan A = \cot B \)   \( \Rightarrow \tan A \times \tan B = 1 \)
  • \( \sin^2 A + \sin^2 B = 1 \)   and   \( \cos^2 A + \cos^2 B = 1 \)

Golden SSC Multipliers:

  • \( \sin \theta \cdot \sin(60^\circ-\theta) \cdot \sin(60^\circ+\theta) = \frac{1}{4} \sin 3\theta \)
  • \( \cos \theta \cdot \cos(60^\circ-\theta) \cdot \cos(60^\circ+\theta) = \frac{1}{4} \cos 3\theta \)
  • \( \tan \theta \cdot \tan(60^\circ-\theta) \cdot \tan(60^\circ+\theta) = \tan 3\theta \)

🎯 SSC CGL Previous Year Questions (PYQs)

Type 1: Golden Multipliers
Q (CGL 2023): What is the value of \( \cos 20^\circ \cos 40^\circ \cos 80^\circ \)?
Solution: Fits the form \( \cos \theta \cdot \cos(60-\theta) \cdot \cos(60+\theta) \) where \( \theta = 20^\circ \). Result = \( \frac{1}{4} \cos 3(20^\circ) = \frac{1}{4} \cos 60^\circ = \frac{1}{4} \times \frac{1}{2} = \frac{1}{8} \).
Type 2: Complementary Angles (\(A+B=90^\circ\))
Q (CGL Tier-2 2022): Evaluate: \( \tan 1^\circ \cdot \tan 2^\circ \cdot \tan 3^\circ \dots \tan 89^\circ \)
Solution: Since \( A + B = 90^\circ \Rightarrow \tan A \cdot \tan B = 1 \). Pairs like \( \tan 1^\circ \tan 89^\circ = 1 \). Only \( \tan 45^\circ \) is left in the middle, which is 1. Answer = 1.
Q (CGL 2021): Find the value of \( \sin^2 10^\circ + \sin^2 20^\circ + \dots + \sin^2 80^\circ + \sin^2 90^\circ \)
Solution: Since \( A + B = 90^\circ \Rightarrow \sin^2 A + \sin^2 B = 1 \). Number of terms from 10 to 80 is 8 terms (4 pairs). So 4 pairs = 4. Plus \( \sin^2 90^\circ = 1 \). Total = 5.
Type 3: Identity Manipulation
Q (CGL 2020): If \( \sec \theta + \tan \theta = P \), what is \( \sec \theta - \tan \theta \)?
Solution: Using identity \( \sec^2 \theta - \tan^2 \theta = 1 \Rightarrow (\sec \theta + \tan \theta)(\sec \theta - \tan \theta) = 1 \). Therefore, \( \sec \theta - \tan \theta = \frac{1}{P} \).
Q (CGL Tier-2): If \( \sec \theta + \tan \theta = 5 \), find \( \sin \theta \).
Solution: \( \sec \theta - \tan \theta = \frac{1}{5} \). Adding them gives \( 2\sec \theta = 5 + \frac{1}{5} = \frac{26}{5} \Rightarrow \sec \theta = \frac{13}{5} \). Hypotenuse = 13, Base = 5. By Pythagoras triplet (5, 12, 13), Perpendicular = 12. Thus \( \sin \theta = \frac{12}{13} \).
Type 4: Max/Min Values
Q (CGL 2019): Find the maximum value of \( 3 \sin \theta + 4 \cos \theta \).
Solution: Format is \( a \sin \theta + b \cos \theta \). Max value = \( \sqrt{a^2 + b^2} = \sqrt{3^2 + 4^2} = \sqrt{25} = 5 \).
Q (CGL 2023): Find the minimum value of \( 4 \tan^2 \theta + 9 \cot^2 \theta \).
Solution: For \( a \tan^2 \theta + b \cot^2 \theta \), the minimum value (using AM-GM inequality) is \( 2\sqrt{ab} = 2\sqrt{4 \times 9} = 2\sqrt{36} = 12 \).
