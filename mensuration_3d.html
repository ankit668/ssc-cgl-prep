📦 Cube & Cuboid

  • • Cube Space Diagonal: d = a√3
  • • Cuboid Space Diagonal: d = √(l² + b² + h²)
  • • Algebraic Relation: (l+b+h)² = d² + TSA
  • • Minimum cuts for N pieces: Factorise N = x·y·z close to ∛N. Total cuts = (x-1)+(y-1)+(z-1)
  • • Water Level Rise: Δh = V_submerged / (Length × Breadth)

🌀 Cylinder & Frustum

  • • Cylinder: V = πr²h, CSA = 2πrh, TSA = 2πr(h+r)
  • • Rolled Sheet: Length becomes 2πr₁ (h₁=B) or Breadth becomes 2πr₂ (h₂=L). V₁/V₂ = L/B
  • • Frustum Volume: V = ⅓πh(R² + r² + Rr)
  • • Frustum Slant Height: l = √[h² + (R - r)²]
  • • Frustum CSA: π(R + r)l; TSA = CSA + π(R² + r²)

⚪ Sphere & Hemisphere

  • • Sphere: V = ⁴⁄₃πr³, SA = 4πr²
  • • Hemisphere: V = ⅔πr³, CSA = 2πr², TSA = 3πr²
  • • Hollow Sphere Shell: V = ⁴⁄₃π(R³ - r³)
  • • Melting N Small Spheres: R = ∛N · r; SA ratio = 1 : ∛N
  • • Cutting Sphere into 2 Hemispheres: Adds 2πr² (TSA becomes 6πr²)

📐 Prism & Pyramid

  • • Prism: V = Base Area × h, LSA = Base Perimeter × h
  • • Pyramid: V = ⅓ × Base Area × h, LSA = ½ × Perimeter × l
  • • Pyramid Slant Height: l² = h² + r_in²
  • • Pyramid Slant Edge: e² = h² + R_circum²
  • • Regular Tetrahedron (side a): h = a√(⅔), V = a³/(6√2), TSA = √3 a²
