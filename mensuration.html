🔴 Advanced SSC Tier-2 Master Formulas

1. Prism & Pyramid (Generic)

Prism
  • Volume: Base Area \( \times \) Height
  • LSA: Base Perimeter \( \times \) Height
  • TSA: LSA \( + 2 \times \) (Base Area)
Pyramid
  • Volume: \( \frac{1}{3} \times \) Base Area \( \times \) Height
  • LSA: \( \frac{1}{2} \times \) Base Perimeter \( \times \) Slant Height
  • TSA: LSA \( + \) Base Area

2. General Triangle Rules

Triangle Centers & Radii
  • Inradius (\( r \)): \( r = \frac{\Delta}{s} \) (Where \( \Delta \) is Area, \( s \) is semi-perimeter)
  • Circumradius (\( R \)): \( R = \frac{abc}{4\Delta} \) (Where \( a,b,c \) are sides)
  • Sine Rule: \( \frac{a}{\sin A} = \frac{b}{\sin B} = \frac{c}{\sin C} = 2R \)
  • Cosine Rule: \( \cos A = \frac{b^2 + c^2 - a^2}{2bc} \)

3. Polygon Formulas (\( n \) = number of sides)

Regular & Irregular Polygons
  • Sum of Interior Angles: \( (n - 2) \times 180^\circ \)
  • Each Interior Angle (Regular): \( \frac{(n - 2) \times 180^\circ}{n} \)
  • Sum of Exterior Angles: \( 360^\circ \) (Always)
  • Each Exterior Angle (Regular): \( \frac{360^\circ}{n} \)
  • Number of Diagonals: \( \frac{n(n - 3)}{2} \)

4. Cutting & Sectioning of 3D Shapes (TCS Favorites)

Cutting a Cone Parallel to Base
  • Cut at Midpoint (2 parts): Ratio of volume of the Top Cone to the Frustum is \( 1 : 7 \).
  • Cut at 3 equal heights: Ratio of volumes from top to bottom is \( 1 : 7 : 19 \). (Pattern: \( 1^3, 2^3-1^3, 3^3-2^3 \)).
Cutting a Sphere Through the Center
  • Every straight cut through the center adds 2 new flat circular faces, increasing TSA by \( 2\pi r^2 \).
  • 1 Cut (2 Hemispheres): Total TSA becomes \( 6\pi r^2 \) (50% increase).
  • 2 Perpendicular Cuts (4 Wedges): Total TSA becomes \( 8\pi r^2 \) (100% increase).
Cutting a Cylinder Parallel to Base
  • Making \( n \) cuts produces \( n+1 \) smaller cylinders.
  • The Total Surface Area increases by \( 2n \times \pi r^2 \).
Cutting a Cube into Smaller Identical Cubes
  • If a large cube of side \( A \) is cut into smaller cubes of side \( a \):
  • Number of smaller cubes \( n = (A/a)^3 \).
  • New Total Surface Area is \( (A/a) \) times the original TSA.

5. Critical SSC CGL Shortcuts & Paths (Time Savers)

Crossroads (Middle Path)
  • Path parallel to length & breadth (Width = \( w \)).
  • Area = \( w(l + b - w) \)
Path Strictly Inside / Outside
  • Path Inside: Area = \( 2w(l + b - 2w) \)
  • Path Outside: Area = \( 2w(l + b + 2w) \)
Common Tangents of Circles (Distance between centers = \( D \))
  • Direct Common Tangent (DCT): \( \sqrt{D^2 - (R - r)^2} \)
  • Transverse Common Tangent (TCT): \( \sqrt{D^2 - (R + r)^2} \)
Area of 4 Walls + Ceiling (For Painting)
  • Total Area = \( 2h(l + b) + lb \)
Euler's Polyhedron Formula
  • \( F + V = E + 2 \) (Faces + Vertices = Edges + 2)
  • (Directly asked in Tier-1 & Tier-2)
Maximum Square inscribed in a Right Triangle
  • Side = \( \frac{P \times B}{P + B} \) (Where P = Perpendicular, B = Base).
Isosceles Triangle Area
  • Area = \( \frac{b}{4}\sqrt{4a^2 - b^2} \) (Where \( a \) = equal sides, \( b \) = base).

Part 2: Exhaustive PYQ Practice Bank (Consolidated)

6. Circle Geometry Rules (Chords & Tangents)

Intersecting Chords Theorem: If two chords AB and CD intersect at an interior point O, then:

\( AO \times OB = CO \times OD \)

Note: The same rule applies if chords intersect outside the circle (Secant-Secant Theorem): \( PA \times PB = PC \times PD \).

1. Triangles & Triangle Centers
Q (CGL 2022): The sides of a triangle are 8 cm, 15 cm, and 17 cm. Find its inradius.
Solution: \( s = \frac{8+15+17}{2} = 20 \). Area = 60. Inradius \( r = \frac{\Delta}{s} = \frac{60}{20} = 3 \) cm. (Shortcut: \( \frac{8+15-17}{2} = 3 \)).
Q (CGL 2023): Find the circumradius of a triangle whose sides are 13 cm, 14 cm, and 15 cm.
Solution: Area \( \Delta = 84 \). Circumradius \( R = \frac{abc}{4\Delta} = \frac{13 \times 14 \times 15}{4 \times 84} = 8.125 \) cm.
Q (CGL Tier-2): The area of an equilateral triangle is \( 400\sqrt{3} \) sq.m. Find its perimeter.
Solution: \( \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}a^2 = 400\sqrt{3} \Rightarrow a^2 = 1600 \Rightarrow a = 40 \) m. Perimeter = \( 3 \times 40 = 120 \) m.
Q (CGL 2021): Find the side of the maximum square that can be inscribed in a right triangle of base 6 cm and height 8 cm.
Solution: Side = \( \frac{P \times B}{P + B} = \frac{8 \times 6}{8 + 6} = \frac{48}{14} = \frac{24}{7} \) cm.
Q (CGL 2020): An isosceles triangle has equal sides of 5 cm and a base of 8 cm. Find its area.
Solution: Area = \( \frac{b}{4}\sqrt{4a^2 - b^2} = \frac{8}{4}\sqrt{4(25) - 64} = 2\sqrt{36} = 12 \) cm².
Q (CGL Tier-2 2018): The lengths of the 3 medians of a triangle are 9, 12, and 15 cm. Find the triangle's area.
Solution: Medians form a right-triplet (9,12,15). Area from medians = \( \frac{1}{2} \times 9 \times 12 = 54 \). Original Area = \( \frac{4}{3} \times 54 = 72 \) cm².
Q (CGL 2019): In \( \Delta ABC \), \( AB = 5 \), \( AC = 8 \), and \( \angle A = 60^\circ \). Find the length of \( BC \).
Solution (Cosine Rule): \( BC^2 = AB^2 + AC^2 - 2(AB)(AC)\cos(60^\circ) = 25 + 64 - 2(40)(\frac{1}{2}) = 89 - 40 = 49 \). \( BC = 7 \).
2. Quadrilaterals & Polygons
Q (CGL 2022): The diagonals of a rhombus are 24 cm and 10 cm. Find its perimeter.
Solution: Side \( a = \frac{1}{2}\sqrt{d_1^2 + d_2^2} = \frac{1}{2}\sqrt{576 + 100} = 13 \) cm. Perimeter = \( 4 \times 13 = 52 \) cm.
Q (CGL 2023): The parallel sides of a trapezium are 20 cm and 10 cm, distance between them is 8 cm. Find area.
Solution: Area = \( \frac{1}{2}(a + b)h = \frac{1}{2}(20 + 10) \times 8 = 15 \times 8 = 120 \) cm².
Q (CGL Tier-1): Ratio of area of regular hexagon to equilateral triangle having the same perimeter?
Solution: Hex side = \( x \), Tri side = \( 2x \). Hex Area = \( 6 \times \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}x^2 \). Tri Area = \( \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}(2x)^2 = \sqrt{3}x^2 \). Ratio = \( \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2} : \sqrt{3} = 3 : 2 \).
Q (CGL 2021): What is the sum of the interior angles of a regular decagon (10 sides)?
Solution: Sum = \( (n - 2) \times 180^\circ = (10 - 2) \times 180 = 1440^\circ \).
Q (CGL 2020): How many diagonals does a regular polygon with 12 sides have?
Solution: Diagonals = \( \frac{n(n - 3)}{2} = \frac{12(12 - 3)}{2} = \frac{12 \times 9}{2} = 54 \).
Q (CGL 2020): Find the area of a regular octagon of side 10 cm.
Solution: Area = \( 2a^2(1 + \sqrt{2}) = 2(100)(1 + \sqrt{2}) = 200(\sqrt{2} + 1) \) cm².
3. Circles, Sectors & Tangents
Q (CGL 2023): The perimeter of a semicircular park is 36 m. Find its radius. (Use \( \pi = 22/7 \))
Solution: Perimeter = \( r(\pi + 2) = r(\frac{22}{7} + 2) = r(\frac{36}{7}) \). \( r(\frac{36}{7}) = 36 \Rightarrow r = 7 \) m.
Q (CGL Tier-2 2022): Distance between centers is 13 cm, radii are 8 cm and 3 cm. Find Direct Common Tangent (DCT).
Solution: DCT = \( \sqrt{D^2 - (R - r)^2} = \sqrt{169 - (8 - 3)^2} = \sqrt{169 - 25} = 12 \) cm.
Q (CGL 2021): Two circles of radii 5 cm and 3 cm touch externally. Find their Transverse Common Tangent (TCT).
Solution: \( D = 5 + 3 = 8 \) cm. TCT = \( \sqrt{D^2 - (R + r)^2} = \sqrt{64 - 64} = 0 \). (Since they touch, TCT crosses the contact point).
Q (CGL 2019): Find the area of a sector of a circle with radius 6 cm if the angle of the sector is \( 60^\circ \).
Solution: Area = \( \frac{\theta}{360}\pi r^2 = \frac{60}{360} \times \pi \times 36 = 6\pi \) cm².
Q (CGL 2021): A wheel makes 1000 revolutions in covering a distance of 88 km. Find the radius of the wheel.
Solution: Distance = \( n \times 2\pi r \Rightarrow 88000 = 1000 \times 2 \times \frac{22}{7} \times r \Rightarrow r = 14 \) m.
4. Cylinders, Cones, Prisms & Pyramids
Q (CGL 2023): The radius of a cylinder is doubled and the height is halved. What is the ratio of the new volume to old?
Solution: Old \( V = \pi r^2 h \). New \( V = \pi (2r)^2 (\frac{h}{2}) = 2\pi r^2 h \). Ratio = \( 2 : 1 \).
Q (CGL 2020): An iron pipe is 21 cm long, exterior diameter 8 cm, thickness 1 cm. Volume of iron?
Solution: Outer \( R = 4 \), Inner \( r = 3 \). Vol = \( \pi h(R^2 - r^2) = \frac{22}{7} \times 21 \times (16 - 9) = 462 \) cm³.
Q (CGL Tier-2): Water flows at 3 km/hr through a pipe of 20 cm internal diameter into a cistern of dia 10 m and depth 2 m. Time?
Solution: Cistern Vol = \( \pi(5^2)(2) = 50\pi \) m³. Pipe rate = \( \pi(0.1)^2 \times 3000 = 30\pi \) m³/hr. Time = \( \frac{50\pi}{30\pi} = \frac{5}{3} \) hours (1 hr 40 mins).
Q (CGL 2022): A road roller (dia 84 cm, length 120 cm) takes 500 revs to level a playground. Area of ground?
Solution: Area = \( n \times 2\pi rh = 500 \times 2 \times \frac{22}{7} \times 0.42 \times 1.2 = 1584 \) m².
Q (CGL 2018): A right prism has an equilateral triangle base of side 8 cm. If its height is 10 cm, find its Volume.
Solution: Volume = Base Area \( \times \) Height = \( (\frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} \times 64) \times 10 = 16\sqrt{3} \times 10 = 160\sqrt{3} \) cm³.
Q (CGL 2019): A pyramid has a square base of side 10 cm and height 12 cm. Find its Total Surface Area.
Solution: Slant height \( l = \sqrt{12^2 + 5^2} = 13 \). LSA = \( \frac{1}{2} \times \text{Perim} \times l = \frac{1}{2}(40)(13) = 260 \). TSA = \( 260 + 100 = 360 \) cm².
5. Frustum & Tetrahedron
Q (CGL Tier-2 2023): A bucket is a frustum of height 21 cm. Radii are 20 cm and 10 cm. Find its volume.
Solution: \( V = \frac{1}{3}\pi h(R^2 + r^2 + Rr) = \frac{1}{3} \times \frac{22}{7} \times 21 \times (400 + 100 + 200) = 22 \times 700 = 15400 \) cm³.
Q (CGL Tier-2 2021): Slant height of a frustum is 5 cm. Difference between radii is 4 cm. Find its height.
Solution: Slant height \( l = \sqrt{h^2 + (R-r)^2} \Rightarrow 5 = \sqrt{h^2 + 4^2} \Rightarrow h = 3 \) cm.
Q (CGL Tier-2 2020): Radii of ends of a frustum are 14 cm and 7 cm, height is 24 cm. Find its Curved Surface Area (CSA).
Solution: \( l = \sqrt{24^2 + 7^2} = 25 \). CSA = \( \pi l(R + r) = \frac{22}{7} \times 25 \times 21 = 66 \times 25 = 1650 \) cm².
Q (CGL 2018): If a solid cone of volume 1600 cm³ is cut at its mid-height parallel to the base, find frustum volume.
Solution: Ratio of top cone to full cone = \( 1^3 : 2^3 = 1 : 8 \). Frustum volume = \( \frac{7}{8} \times 1600 = 1400 \) cm³.
Q (CGL Tier-2): Find the total surface area and volume of a regular tetrahedron of side 6 cm.
Solution: TSA = \( \sqrt{3}a^2 = \sqrt{3}(36) = 36\sqrt{3} \) cm². Volume = \( \frac{\sqrt{2}}{12}a^3 = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{12}(216) = 18\sqrt{2} \) cm³.
6. Spheres, Hemispheres & Polyhedrons
Q (CGL 2023): A solid metallic sphere of radius 8 cm is melted into small spheres of radius 2 cm. How many spheres?
Solution: Number of spheres \( = \frac{R^3}{r^3} = \frac{8^3}{2^3} = 4^3 = 64 \).
Q (CGL 2021): A spherical shell has outer radius 9 cm and inner radius 8 cm. Find volume of metal.
Solution: Volume \( = \frac{4}{3}\pi (R^3 - r^3) = \frac{4}{3}\pi (729 - 512) = \frac{868\pi}{3} \) cm³.
Q (CGL 2019): A solid sphere is cut into two identical hemispheres. What is the % increase in total surface area?
Solution: Orig TSA = \( 4\pi r^2 \). New TSA = \( 6\pi r^2 \). Increase = \( 2\pi r^2 \). % increase = \( \frac{2}{4} \times 100 = 50\% \).
Q (CGL 2018): A polyhedron has 8 faces and 12 edges. How many vertices does it have?
Solution (Euler's Formula): \( F + V = E + 2 \Rightarrow 8 + V = 12 + 2 \Rightarrow V = 14 - 8 = 6 \) vertices.
7. Advanced: Cutting Shapes & Rectangular Paths
Q (CGL Tier-2 2023): A right circular cone is cut into 3 equal parts by 2 planes parallel to the base. Ratio of volumes?
Solution: Heights ratio \( 1 : 2 : 3 \). Cone volumes \( 1^3 : 2^3 : 3^3 = 1 : 8 : 27 \). Sliced pieces = \( 1 : (8-1) : (27-8) = 1 : 7 : 19 \).
Q (CGL Tier-2 2021): A solid cylinder is cut by 4 planes parallel to its base. By what absolute amount does TSA increase?
Solution: 4 cuts produce 5 pieces. Each cut exposes 2 new bases (\( 2\pi r^2 \)). Total increase = \( 4 \times 2\pi r^2 = 8\pi r^2 \).
Q (CGL 2023): A rectangular park is 60m long and 40m wide. A path of width 2m is strictly inside. Find path area.
Solution: Area = \( 2w(l + b - 2w) = 2(2)(60 + 40 - 4) = 4(96) = 384 \) m².
Q (CGL 2022): A path 3m wide runs outside and around a garden of 20m by 15m. Find path area.
Solution: Area = \( 2w(l + b + 2w) = 2(3)(20 + 15 + 6) = 6(41) = 246 \) m².
Q (CGL Tier-2 2021): Two perpendicular paths of width 5m cross the middle of an 80m x 60m field. Find path area.
Solution: Area = \( w(l + b - w) = 5(80 + 60 - 5) = 5(135) = 675 \) m².
Q (CGL 2020): Room dimensions are 5m, 4m, 3m (h). Find area of walls + ceiling for white-washing.
Solution: Area = \( 2h(l + b) + lb = 2(3)(5 + 4) + (5 \times 4) = 54 + 20 = 74 \) m².
8. Circle Chords (Geometry/Mensuration Hybrid)
Q (CGL 2023): Two chords AB and CD of a circle intersect at O inside the circle. If AO = 6 cm, OB = 4 cm, and CO = 3 cm, find the length of OD.
Solution: By Intersecting Chords Theorem: \( AO \times OB = CO \times OD \).
\( 6 \times 4 = 3 \times OD \Rightarrow 24 = 3 \times OD \Rightarrow OD = 8 \) cm.
Q (CGL Tier-2): Chords AB and CD intersect externally at P. If PA = 12 cm, PB = 4 cm, and PC = 8 cm, find the length of CD.
Solution: By Secant Theorem: \( PA \times PB = PC \times PD \).
\( 12 \times 4 = 8 \times PD \Rightarrow 48 = 8 \times PD \Rightarrow PD = 6 \) cm.
Since \( PD = 6 \) and \( PC = 8 \) (P is outside, C is closer to P? Wait, usually Secant is PA = whole secant, PB = outer part).
Assume PA is whole, PB outer. \( 12 \times 4 = 48 \). For CD, if PC is whole secant (8) and PD is outer (6), then CD = PC - PD = 8 - 6 = 2 cm.
9. Sphere V & S Relation Shortcut
Q (CGL 2022): The surface area of a sphere is 616 cm². What is its volume in terms of its radius \(r\)?
Solution: Instead of calculating the radius fully, use the direct relation \( V = \frac{r}{3} \times S \).
Substitute \( S = 616 \): \( V = \frac{r}{3} \times 616 = \frac{616r}{3} \) cm³.
Q (CGL 2021): If the numerical value of the volume of a sphere is twice the numerical value of its surface area, find the radius.
Solution: Given \( V = 2S \). Using the shortcut \( V = \frac{r}{3} \times S \), we substitute V:
\( 2S = \frac{r}{3} \times S \). The 'S' cancels out on both sides, leaving \( 2 = \frac{r}{3} \Rightarrow r = 6 \) units.
