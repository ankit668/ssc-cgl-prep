🔥 Rajbhasha Extended Cheat Sheet (All 70 Qs)

📜 1. Important Articles & Amendments

  • Art 343(1): Hindi in Devanagari is Official Language.
  • Art 345: States empowered to adopt their official languages.
  • Art 348: Language of Supreme Court & High Courts.
  • Art 350A: Instruction in mother-tongue at primary stage.
  • Art 350B: Special Officer for Linguistic Minorities.
  • Art 351: Directives for development of Hindi.
  • 8th Schedule: Has 22 languages (Originally 14).
  • 21st Amend (1967): Added Sindhi (15th).
  • 71st Amend (1992): Added Konkani, Manipuri, Nepali.
  • 92nd Amend (2003): Added Bodo, Dogri, Maithili, Santhali.

🏛️ 2. Acts, Rules & Regions

  • OL Act: Passed 1963, amended 1967.
  • Sec 3(3) of OL Act: 14 types of documents must be bilingual.
  • Sec 8: Central Govt has power to make rules.
  • OL Rules 1976: Apply to whole of India EXCEPT Tamil Nadu.
  • Region A: UP, MP, Bihar, Rajasthan, Haryana, HP, Uttarakhand, Jharkhand, Chhattisgarh, Delhi, A&N.
  • Region B: Gujarat, Maharashtra, Punjab, Chandigarh, Daman & Diu, D&NH.
  • Region C: Everywhere else (South, East, North-East).

📏 3. Rule Compliance & Targets

  • Rule 5: Letters received in Hindi MUST be replied in Hindi.
  • Rule 8: File notes can be written in Either Hindi or English.
  • Rule 9: Definition of 'Working Knowledge' of Hindi.
  • Rule 10(4): Offices where 80% or more staff know Hindi are declared as 'Notified Offices'.
  • Rule 11: Manuals, codes, forms must be bilingual.
  • Rule 12: Head of Office is responsible for compliance.
  • Displays: Stamps, Name Plates, Sign Boards must be bilingual with Hindi on TOP, English at bottom.
  • Region A Target: 100% inter-office communication in Hindi.
  • Check Points: Setup to ensure 100% compliance of Sec 3(3).

🎓 4. Training Courses

  • Hierarchy: Prabodh → Praveen → Pragya → Parangat.
  • Prabodh: Elementary course for non-Hindi areas.
  • Pragya: Considered equivalent to Matriculation level.
  • Parangat: Advanced working-knowledge course focusing on workplace communication.
  • CHTI: Conducts Hindi Typing/Stenography training.

💼 5. Committees & Ministry

  • Parliamentary Committee: 30 members (20 LS, 10 RS). Formed 1976. Has 3 Sub-Committees. Submitted 12 volumes. Chairman is traditionally the Union Home Minister.
  • 1st Sub-Committee: Inspects Central Ministries/Departments.
  • Kendriya Hindi Samiti: Chaired by the Prime Minister.
  • TOLIC: Constituted in towns with 10 or more Central offices.
  • OLIC: Official Language Implementation Committee meets Quarterly.
  • Ministry: Dept of Official Language is under Ministry of Home Affairs (created 1975).

🏆 6. Awards & Important Dates

  • Hindi Diwas: September 14 (Adopted in 1949).
  • World Hindi Day: January 10. First World Hindi Conference was in Nagpur, 1975.
  • Rajbhasha Gaurav Puraskar: For writing original books in Hindi on Rajbhasha topics.
  • Rajbhasha Kirti Puraskar: Top award for Ministries/Depts for outstanding implementation.
  • Incentive Scheme: Operational cycle is 1st April to 31st March.
  • Annual Programme: Issued yearly by Dept of Official Language.
