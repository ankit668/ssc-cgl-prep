🔥 Rajbhasha 2-Minute Cheat Sheet

📜 1. Constitutional Provisions

  • Article 343(1): Hindi in Devanagari script is the Official Language.
  • Article 351: Directives for development of Hindi.
  • Part XVII (17): Deals with Official Language.
  • 8th Schedule: Has 22 languages (Originally 14).
  • 21st Amendment (1967): Added Sindhi (15th).
  • 92nd Amendment (2003): Added Bodo, Dogri, Maithili, Santhali.

🏛️ 2. Acts & Rules

  • OL Act: Passed 1963, amended 1967.
  • Sec 3(3) of OL Act: 14 types of documents (General Orders, Resolutions, Contracts) must be bilingual.
  • Sec 8: Central Govt has power to make rules.
  • Rule 5: Letters received in Hindi MUST be replied in Hindi.
  • Rule 8: Notes on files can be written in Either Hindi or English.
  • Rule 11: Manuals, codes, forms must be bilingual.
  • Rule 12: Head of Office is responsible for compliance.

🗺️ 3. The 3 Regions

  • Region A: UP, MP, Bihar, Rajasthan, Haryana, HP, Uttarakhand, Jharkhand, Chhattisgarh, Delhi, A&N.
  • Region B: Gujarat, Maharashtra, Punjab, Chandigarh, Daman & Diu, Dadra & Nagar Haveli.
  • Region C: Everywhere else (Andhra Pradesh, TN, Kerala, Karnataka, WB, North-East).

🎓 4. Training & Committees

  • Hierarchy: Prabodh → Praveen → Pragya → Parangat.
  • Prabodh: Elementary course for non-Hindi areas.
  • CHTI: Conducts Hindi Typing/Stenography training.
  • Parliamentary Committee: 30 members (20 LS, 10 RS). Formed 1976. Has 3 Sub-Committees. Submitted 12 volumes.
  • Ministry: Dept of Official Language is under Ministry of Home Affairs.

🏆 5. Awards & Dates

  • Hindi Diwas: September 14 (Adopted in 1949).
  • World Hindi Day: January 10.
  • Rajbhasha Gaurav Puraskar: For writing original books in Hindi on Rajbhasha.
  • Annual Programme: Issued yearly by Dept of Official Language.
