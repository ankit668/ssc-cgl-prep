Overseas Citizen of India (OCI)
The Overseas Citizen of India (OCI) Scheme was introduced
in 2005 through an amendment to the Citizenship Act, 1955.
Note: The Person of Indian Origin (PIO) Scheme and the
Overseas Citizen of India (OCI) Scheme were merged in
2015 to simplify the system for people of Indian origin
living abroad.
Restrictions on OCI cardholders:
1) No right to vote in elections.
2) Not eligible for public services or government posts.
3) Not permitted to own agricultural property in India.
4) Indian passport is not issued to OCI holders, as they are
foreign citizens.
Benefits for OCI cardholders:
1) Lifelong multiple-entry visa to visit India.
2) Facilities similar to NRIs in economic, financial, and
educational fields.
3) Permission to buy or sell immovable property, except
agricultural land, plantation property, or farmhouses.
4) Eligible to practice certain profession.
