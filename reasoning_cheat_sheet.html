📅 1. CALENDAR CODES

  • Formula: (Date + Month Code + Year + (Year/4) + Century Code) ÷ 7 (Find Remainder)
  • Normal Year: 144 - 025 - 036 - 146 (Jan to Dec)
  • Leap Year: Jan = 0, Feb = 3 (rest are the same)
  • Century Codes: 1800s = 2 | 1900s = 0 | 2000s = 6
  • Day Codes: Sun=1, Mon=2, Tue=3, Wed=4, Thu=5, Fri=6, Sat=0

📅 Advanced Calendar Patterns & Tricks

  • Year Repetition Trick: When will the same calendar repeat? Look at the remainder of `(Year ÷ 4)`:
    • If Remainder = 1 → Repeats after 6 years (e.g., 2017 repeats in 2023)
    • If Remainder = 2 or 3 → Repeats after 11 years (e.g., 2018 repeats in 2029)
    • If Remainder = 0 (Leap Year) → Repeats after 28 years (e.g., 2020 repeats in 2048)
  • Same Months in a Year: Months with the same code have identical calendars!
    • Normal Year: (Jan & Oct), (Feb, Mar & Nov), (Apr & Jul), (Sep & Dec)
    • Leap Year: (Jan, Apr & Jul), (Feb & Aug), (Mar & Nov), (Sep & Dec)
  • Century Day Rules:
    • The Last Day of a century can only be: Sunday, Monday, Wednesday, or Friday. (It can NEVER be Tue, Thu, or Sat).
🔥 Top 8 Recent PYQs - Calendars
[SSC CGL 2023] What was the day of the week on 15th August 1947?
Solution: 15 + 3 (Aug) + 47 (Yr) + 11 (Leaps) + 0 = 76. 76 ÷ 7 leaves Remainder 6. Code 6 = Friday.
[SSC CGL 2022] The calendar for the year 2017 will be the same for the year?
Solution: Divide year 2017 by 4. (17 ÷ 4) leaves remainder 1. Year repetition rule: Remainder 1 means add 6 years. 2017 + 6 = 2023.
[SSC CHSL 2021] Which of the following two months in a non-leap year have the same calendar?
Solution: Months with the same month-code have the same calendar. In a normal year (144-025-036-146), April is 0 and July is 0. So, April and July.
[RRB NTPC 2021] Which of the following days can never be the last day of a century?
Solution: Century endings strictly follow the 5, 3, 1, 0 code rule (Friday, Wednesday, Monday, Sunday). So they can never be Tuesday, Thursday, or Saturday.
[RRB NTPC 2022] What was the day of the week on 26th January 1950?
Solution: 26 + 1 (Jan) + 50 (Yr) + 12 (Leaps) + 0 = 89. 89 ÷ 7 leaves Remainder 5. Code 5 = Thursday.
[SSC CHSL 2022] What was the day of the week on 2nd October 1869?
Solution: 2 + 1 (Oct) + 69 (Yr) + 17 (Leaps) + 2 (1800s) = 91. 91 ÷ 7 leaves Remainder 0. Code 0 = Saturday.
[IBPS PO 2023] What will be the day of the week on 5th June 2023?
Solution: 5 + 5 (Jun) + 23 (Yr) + 5 (Leaps) + 6 (2000s) = 44. 44 ÷ 7 leaves Remainder 2. Code 2 = Monday.
[SBI Clerk 2021] What was the day of the week on 31st December 1999?
Solution: 31 + 6 (Dec) + 99 (Yr) + 24 (Leaps) + 0 = 160. 160 ÷ 7 leaves Remainder 6. Code 6 = Friday.

⏰ 2. CLOCKS

  • Angle Between Hands: θ = |(11/2 × Minutes) - (30 × Hours)|
  • Hands Overlapping (0°): (60/11 × H) minutes past H
  • Mirror Image Time: Subtract the given time from 11:60
🔥 Top 5 Recent PYQs - Clocks
[RRB Group D 2022] At what angle are the hands inclined at 4:20?
Solution: Angle formula: |(11/2 × 20) - (30 × 4)| = |110 - 120| = |-10| = 10°.
[SSC CGL 2023] At what time between 3 and 4 o'clock will the hands of a watch coincide/overlap (0°)?
Solution: H = 3. Formula: 60/11 × 3 = 180/11 = 16 4/11. Ans: 16 4/11 min past 3.
[RRB NTPC 2021] At what time between 8 and 9 o'clock will the hands overlap?
Solution: H = 8. Formula: 60/11 × 8 = 480/11 = 43 7/11. Ans: 43 7/11 min past 8.
[IBPS PO 2022] At what time between 2 and 3 o'clock are the hands of a clock exactly together?
Solution: H = 2. Formula: 60/11 × 2 = 120/11 = 10 10/11. Ans: 10 10/11 min past 2.
[IBPS Clerk 2023] What is the mirror image of a clock showing 8:40?
Solution: Subtract from 11:60. (11 - 8):(60 - 40) = 3:20.

🔠 3. ALPHABETS & CODING

  • Reverse Position: 27 - Normal Position
  • Opposite Pairs: A-Z B-Y C-X D-W E-V F-U G-T H-S I-R J-Q K-P L-O M-N
🔥 Top 5 Recent PYQs - Alphabets
[IBPS PO 2023] If 'CAT' = 24, then 'DOG' = ?
Solution: C(3) + A(1) + T(20) = 24. D(4) + O(15) + G(7) = 26.
[SSC CHSL 2023] If 'GO' = 32 and 'SHE' = 49, what is 'SOME'?
Solution: Uses opposite letters. G=T(20), O=L(12) sum=32. SOME -> H(8) + L(12) + N(14) + V(22) = 56.
[RRB NTPC 2022] What is the sum of alphabetical positions in "WATER"?
Solution: W(23) + A(1) + T(20) + E(5) + R(18) = 67.
[SSC CGL 2022] If 'BAT' is coded as 'YZG', how is 'CAT' coded?
Solution: Opposite pairs. B->Y, A->Z, T->G. Therefore C->X, A->Z, T->G. Ans: XZG.
[SBI PO 2021] What is the reverse alphabetical position of the letter 'K'?
Solution: Normal position = 11. Reverse = 27 - 11 = 16.

🎲 4. PAINTED CUBES

  • (Where n = large edge ÷ small edge)
  • 3 Sides Painted (Corners): Always 8
  • 2 Sides Painted (Edges): 12(n - 2)
  • 1 Side Painted (Faces): 6(n - 2)²
  • 0 Sides Painted (Inside): (n - 2)³
🔥 Top 5 Recent PYQs - Cubes
[SSC CGL 2023] A cube is cut into 27 smaller cubes. How many have exactly 3 painted faces?
Solution: n = 3. Cubes with 3 painted faces are always the corners. Ans: 8.
[SSC CHSL 2022] A solid red cube is cut into 64 smaller cubes. How many have exactly 2 painted faces?
Solution: n³ = 64, so n = 4. Formula: 12(n - 2) = 12(4 - 2) = 24.
[RRB ALP 2024] A cube is cut into 125 smaller cubes. How many have exactly 1 painted face?
Solution: n³ = 125, so n = 5. Formula: 6(n - 2)² = 6(3)² = 6 × 9 = 54.
[Delhi Police 2023] A cube is cut into 216 smaller cubes. How many have NO painted faces (unpainted)?
Solution: n³ = 216, so n = 6. Formula: (n - 2)³ = (6 - 2)³ = 4³ = 64.
[UPSSSC 2022] A 10cm painted cube is cut into 2cm cubes. How many have exactly 2 painted faces?
Solution: n = 10/2 = 5. Formula: 12(n - 2) = 12(3) = 36.

🔺 5. COUNTING FIGURES

  • Triangles inside a Square (with cross): Number of small inner triangles × 2
  • Triangles inside a Triangle (base cut into n parts): n(n+1) ÷ 2
🔥 Top 5 Recent PYQs - Figure Counting
[SSC CGL 2023] How many triangles are in a square box with both diagonals drawn?
Solution: Counting the basic small inner triangles gives 4. Formula: 4 × 2 = 8.
[RRB NTPC 2022] A large triangle has its base divided by vertical lines into 4 sections. Total triangles?
Solution: n = 4. Formula: n(n+1)÷2 = 4(5)÷2 = 10.
[SSC GD 2023] How many triangles in a square crossed by 2 diagonals and horizontal/vertical bisectors?
Solution: The number of small inner triangles is 8. Formula: 8 × 2 = 16.
[IB ACIO 2021] A triangle's base is divided into 5 sections by straight lines from the apex. Total triangles?
Solution: n = 5. Formula: 5(6)÷2 = 30÷2 = 15.
[SSC CHSL 2023] A triangle's base is divided into 6 sections. Find the total number of triangles.
Solution: n = 6. Formula: 6(7)÷2 = 42÷2 = 21.

📈 6. RANKING & ORDERING

  • Total Persons: (Position from Left/Top) + (Position from Right/Bottom) - 1
  • New Position (When 2 people swap): New pos = Old pos + Difference in positions of the other person.
  • Middle Persons (Normal): Total - (Left + Right) (When Total > L+R)
  • Middle Persons (Overlap): (Left + Right) - Total - 2 (When Total < L+R)
🔥 Top 7 Recent PYQs - Ranking
[SSC CGL 2023] In a row, Ram is 15th from left and 20th from right. Total boys?
Solution: Total = (Left + Right) - 1 = (15 + 20) - 1 = 34.
[IBPS Clerk 2022] A class has 45 students. Neha is 12th from top. Her rank from bottom?
Solution: Bottom = Total - Top + 1 = 45 - 12 + 1 = 34th.
[RRB NTPC 2021] In a row of 50 people, A is 10th from left, B is 15th from right. How many between them?
Solution: Normal case (50 > 10+15). Between = 50 - (10 + 15) = 50 - 25 = 25.
[SBI PO 2023] In a row of 30, A is 20th from left, B is 18th from right. How many between them?
Solution: Overlap case (30 < 20+18). Between = (20 + 18) - 30 - 2 = 38 - 32 = 6.
[SSC CHSL 2022] A is 10th from left, B is 15th from right. They swap. A becomes 18th from left. Total?
Solution: A's new left (18) + B's old right (15) - 1 = 18 + 15 - 1 = 32.
[SSC CGL 2024] In a row, P is 12th from left and Q is 17th from right. When they swap, P becomes 27th from left. What is Q's new position from right?
Solution: Use Difference Trick: P's position changed from 12 to 27 (Difference = +15). Therefore, Q's position will also change by exactly +15. Q's new right = 17 + 15 = 32nd.
[IBPS PO 2023] Sita is 9th from left and Gita is 13th from right. They swap positions and Sita becomes 18th from left. What is Gita's new position from right? And what is the total number of girls?
Solution: Gita's New Pos: Sita jumped from 9 to 18 (+9). So Gita jumps 13 + 9 = 22nd.
Total: Sita's New Left (18) + Gita's Old Right (13) - 1 = 30.

🎲 7. DICE RULES

  • Standard Dice: Sum of opposite faces = 7 (1↔6, 2↔5, 3↔4)
  • One Common Face Rule: Write numbers clockwise starting from the common face. Number pairs that align are opposites.
  • Two Common Faces Rule: The remaining 3rd faces in both dice are opposite to each other.
  • Open Dice Rule: Alternate faces in a straight line are opposite.
🔥 Top 5 Recent PYQs - Dice
[SSC GD 2023] Two positions of a dice show: (2, 3, 5) and (2, 4, 6). Opposite to 3?
Solution: Common is 2. Clockwise 1st: 2→3→5. Clockwise 2nd: 2→4→6. 3 aligns with 4. Ans: 4.
[RRB Group D 2022] Dice shows: (1, 5, 6) and (1, 3, 5). What is opposite to 6?
Solution: 1 and 5 are common in both. The remaining faces are 6 and 3. Ans: 3.
[Delhi Police 2023] In a standard dice, what is opposite to 2?
Solution: Standard dice rule: sum = 7. 7 - 2 = 5.
[SSC MTS 2021] Open dice sequence: 1-2-3-4 in a line. What is opposite to 2?
Solution: Alternate faces are opposite. 1 is opposite 3, 2 is opposite 4. Ans: 4.
[IB ACIO 2022] Dice shows (A, B, C) and (C, D, E). Opposite to B?
Solution: Common is C. Clockwise 1: C→A→B. Clockwise 2: C→D→E. B aligns with E. Ans: E.

🧭 8. DIRECTION & DISTANCE

  • Shortest Distance: Hypotenuse = √(Base² + Height²) (Pythagoras Theorem)
  • Turns: Right Turn = 90° Clockwise | Left Turn = 90° Anti-Clockwise
  • Sunlight (Morning): Sun is in East. Shadows fall to the West.
  • Sunlight (Evening): Sun is in West. Shadows fall to the East.
🔥 Top 5 Recent PYQs - Direction
[SBI PO 2023] A goes 3km North, turns right and goes 4km. Shortest distance from start?
Solution: Base=4, Height=3. Distance = √(3² + 4²) = √(9 + 16) = √25 = 5 km.
[SSC CGL 2022] A walks 10m South, turns left walks 10m, turns left walks 10m. Direction from start?
Solution: South 10m -> Left(East) 10m -> Left(North) 10m. He is exactly East of start.
[RRB NTPC 2021] One morning, A and B are facing each other. A's shadow falls exactly to A's left. Which way is B facing?
Solution: Morning = Sun in East. Shadow falls in West. A's left is West, so A faces North. B faces South.
[IBPS Clerk 2022] A man faces West. Turns 45° clockwise, then 180° clockwise. Which direction?
Solution: Total turn = 45 + 180 = 225° clockwise. West + 90(N) + 90(E) + 45 = North-East.
[SSC CHSL 2023] Goes 5km East, turns right 4km, turns left 5km. Shortest distance?
Solution: Total horizontal = 5+5=10. Vertical = 4. Distance = √(10² + 4²) = √(100 + 16) = √116 km.

🩸 9. BLOOD RELATIONS

  • Symbols Trick: Male = [+] | Female = [-]
  • Connections: Married = A ══ B | Siblings = A ── B
  • Generations: Use a vertical line │ with older generations on top.
  • Coded Relations: (e.g., A $ B % C) Always draw the family tree backwards (from Right to Left) to save time!
🔥 Top 5 Recent PYQs - Blood Relations
[SBI PO 2023] Pointing to a boy, Suresh said, "He is the son of the only son of my mother." How is Suresh related to the boy?
Solution: Suresh's mother's only son = Suresh himself. The boy is the son of Suresh. Ans: Father.
[SSC CGL 2022] A is B's sister. C is B's mother. D is C's father. How is A related to D?
Solution: A and B are siblings. C is their mother. D is C's father (so D is A's maternal grandfather). Ans: Granddaughter.
[IBPS Clerk 2022] A+B = Father, A-B = Brother, A%B = Wife. Relation in P + Q - R % S?
Solution: Right to left: R is wife of S. Q is brother of R. P is father of Q. So P is father of R, making P the father-in-law of S. Ans: P is father-in-law of S.
[RRB NTPC 2021] Pointing to a woman, a man says, "She is the only daughter of my wife's grandfather." How is the woman related to the man?
Solution: Wife's grandfather's only daughter = wife's mother. Man's wife's mother is his mother-in-law. Ans: Mother-in-law.
[SSC CHSL 2023] X and Y are brothers. R is father of Y. S is maternal uncle of X and brother of T. How is T related to R?
Solution: R is father of X and Y. S is maternal uncle (mother's brother). Since S is T's brother, T is the mother. T is married to R. Ans: Wife.

🔢 8. NUMBER SERIES, MATRIX & CODING TRICKS

  • Coding-Decoding - Opposite Letters: Remember the pairs whose positional values sum to 27!
    A-Z (Azad), B-Y (Boy), C-X (Crux), D-W (Dew), E-V (Even), F-U (Full), G-T (GT Road), H-S (High School), I-R (Indian Railways), J-Q (Jungle Queen), K-P (Kanpur), L-O (Love), M-N (Man)
  • Coding-Decoding - Vowel/Consonant Shift: Recent trend: Vowels are shifted differently (+1) than Consonants (-1). Always check vowels!
  • Number Series Golden Rules:
    • Grows slowly? → Check Step Differences (Double differences are very common).
    • Grows rapidly? → Check Multiplication or Squares/Cubes (e.g., n² ± k or n³ ± k).
    • Goes up and down? → Check Alternating Series (Two separate series interleaved).
  • Missing Number Matrices: Always check row-wise or column-wise depending on where the largest numbers are. Top patterns: R3 = R1 + R2, R3 = R1 × R2, R3 = R1² + R2², or R3 = (R1 + R2) ÷ 2.
