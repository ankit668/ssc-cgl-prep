🏛️ Constitutional Articles & Key Amendments
Polity
Article / AmendmentCrucial Exam Fact (Direct & Sister)
61st CAA (1988)Lowered voting age from 21 to 18 years for Lok Sabha & Vidhan Sabha elections (Amended Article 326). Came into force 28 March 1989.
42nd CAA (1976)Known as the "Mini-Constitution". Added words "Socialist, Secular, Integrity" to Preamble. Added Fundamental Duties (Part IVA, Article 51A) on recommendation of Swaran Singh Committee.
44th CAA (1978)Removed Right to Property from Fundamental Rights (made ordinary Legal Right under Article 300A). Replaced "internal disturbance" with "armed rebellion" in Article 352.
86th CAA (2002)Inserted Article 21A (Free & compulsory education for children 6–14 years as Fundamental Right). Added 11th Fundamental Duty (51A-k). RTE Act passed in 2009.
91st CAA (2003)Capped Council of Ministers at 15% of Lok Sabha / Legislative Assembly total strength. Minimum 12 in states.
Article 110Money Bill: Speaker of Lok Sabha decides finally whether a bill is a Money Bill or not. Rajya Sabha can delay for max 14 days.
Article 112Annual Financial Statement (popularly known as the Union Budget). Presented by Finance Minister.
Article 113Procedure regarding estimates: Demands for Grants are voted exclusively in the Lok Sabha. Rajya Sabha has no voting power on demands.
Article 114Appropriation Bill: No money shall be withdrawn from the Consolidated Fund of India except under appropriation made by law.
Article 148Comptroller and Auditor General of India (CAG): Guardian of public purse. Submits audit reports to President under Article 151.
Article 280Finance Commission: Quasi-judicial body constituted every 5 years by President. 16th Finance Commission Chairman is Dr. Arvind Panagariya.
Article 312All-India Services: Rajya Sabha alone can initiate creation of new All-India Services by a 2/3rd resolution.
Article 324Superintendence, direction and control of elections vested in the Election Commission of India.
Article 356President's Rule (State Emergency). Maximum duration permissible with Parliamentary approval is 3 years.
BNS / BNSS ChangesCame into force on 1 July 2024:
• BNS Sec 152: Replaced Sedition (IPC 124A) with "Acts endangering sovereignty, unity and integrity of India".
• BNS Sec 69: Sexual intercourse on deceitful promise to marry / deceitful employment.
• BNS Sec 103(2): Mob lynching by 5+ persons penalized by death or life imprisonment.
• BNSS Sec 479: Bail for first-time undertrial offenders after serving 1/3rd maximum imprisonment.

🌍 Geography, Mountain Passes & Minerals
Geography
Geographical EntityExact Exam Data
Kashmir Valley LocationLies between the Great Himalayas (Himadri) to the northeast and the Pir Panjal Range (Middle/Lesser Himalayas) to the southwest.
Pir Panjal & Banihal PassBanihal Pass cuts across the Pir Panjal Range connecting Jammu to Srinagar. It houses the famous Jawahar Tunnel.
Copper in MPMalanjkhand Copper Belt in Balaghat district of Madhya Pradesh produces over 50% of India's copper ore (operated by Hindustan Copper Ltd).
Bauxite in MPFound abundantly in the Amarkantak Plateau, Maikala range, and Katni/Jabalpur districts. (Odisha is #1 overall producer in India).
Diamonds in MPPanna Diamond Mines (Majhgawan pipe) in MP is the only active mechanized diamond mine in India, operated by NMDC.
Gold in IndiaHutti Gold Mines (Raichur, Karnataka) is currently the only active primary gold-producing mine in India. Kolar (KGF) is historic.
Kuno National ParkLocated in Sheopur district, Madhya Pradesh. Selected for Project Cheetah (reintroduction from Namibia in Sep 2022 and South Africa in Feb 2023).
India's 1st Dugong ReserveEstablished in Palk Bay, Tamil Nadu (protects herbivorous marine mammal Dugong / Sea Cow).
Keibul Lamjao National ParkWorld's only floating national park, located on Loktak Lake, Manipur. Famous for floating decomposed plant masses called Phumdis and the endangered Sangai deer (Rucervus eldii).
Highest Peak in South IndiaAnamudi (2,695 m) in the Anaimalai Hills, Kerala (also highest in Western Ghats). Doddabetta (2,637 m) is highest in Nilgiris.
Highest Peak in Eastern GhatsJindhagada Peak (1,690 m) in Araku Valley, Andhra Pradesh (followed by Arma Konda).
📈 Economy, 1991 Reforms & Banking History
Economy
Economic MilestoneKey Exam Facts
1991 LPG ReformsPrime Minister was P. V. Narasimha Rao; Finance Minister was Dr. Manmohan Singh.
• Policy: Liberalisation, Privatisation, and Globalisation.
• Triggered by Balance of Payments (BoP) crisis with foreign reserves falling to barely 2–3 weeks of imports.
Rupee Devaluation 1991Conducted in two quick steps on 1 July 1991 (~9%) and 3 July 1991 (~11%) to boost exports and stem capital flight.
SEBI Statutory StatusSEBI was created in 1988 as non-statutory body, but gained full statutory regulatory powers via the SEBI Act, 1992 following the Harshad Mehta securities scam.
1st Bank Nationalisation (1969)On 19 July 1969, PM Indira Gandhi nationalised 14 major commercial banks having deposits of ₹50 crore or more.
2nd Bank Nationalisation (1980)On 15 April 1980, 6 more banks with deposits of ₹200 crore or more were nationalised.
NITI AayogEstablished on 1 January 2015 replacing the Planning Commission. Chairperson: Prime Minister. Ex-officio Think Tank.
16th Finance CommissionConstituted under Article 280 for recommendations covering 2026–2031. Chairman: Dr. Arvind Panagariya (former Vice-Chairman of NITI Aayog).
🎨 Art, Culture, Festivals & Music Maestros
Culture
Cultural ElementCore Details & Associations
Hornbill FestivalKnown as the "Festival of Festivals". Celebrated annually from 1 to 10 December at Kisama Heritage Village near Kohima, Nagaland. Named after the great Indian hornbill bird.
Wangala FestivalThe famous "100 Drums Festival" of the Garo tribe in Meghalaya. A post-harvest thanksgiving festival dedicated to Misi Saljong (Sun God).
Chapchar KutCelebrated in Mizoram in March upon completion of clearing bamboo forests for Jhum (shifting) cultivation. Famous for Cheraw (bamboo dance).
Baul Folk MusicMystic minstrel folk songs of West Bengal & Bangladesh. Recognized by UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage. Lead instruments: Ektara (single-string drone) and Dotara.
Sarod MaestrosLegendary exponents: Pt. Buddhadev Das Gupta, Ustad Ali Akbar Khan, Ustad Amjad Ali Khan.
Santoor MaestrosPt. Shivkumar Sharma, Bhajan Sopori (classical Kashmiri stringed instrument played with wooden mallets).
Shehnai MaestroUstad Bismillah Khan (Bharat Ratna 2001, played from Red Fort on 15 August 1947).
Hoysala Temples (42nd UNESCO Site)Carved out of Chloritic Schist (Soapstone) at Belur, Halebidu, and Somanathapura in Karnataka (12th–13th century stellate star-shaped platform).

ConceptExact Exam Principle
Pascal's PrinciplePressure applied to an enclosed fluid is transmitted equally in all directions.
• Direct Applications: Hydraulic Brakes, Hydraulic Lifts, Hydraulic Press.
Watson-Crick DNA Model (1953)Nitrogenous base pairs held by Hydrogen Bonds.
• Adenine = Thymine (2 Hydrogen bonds).
• Guanine ≡ Cytosine (3 Hydrogen bonds).
• Backbone held by: Phosphodiester bonds.

Phosphorus Allotropes• White Phosphorus: Highly toxic, chemiluminescent, catches fire spontaneously in air, stored under water.
• Red Phosphorus: Stable, non-toxic, used in matchstick heads with sand & glue.
